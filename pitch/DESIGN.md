# Liensight pitch: design system
Concept: one continuous sky world (the landing page), a camera that flies through it; scenes change the weather (sky -> ink for the problem -> sky -> red for the fit).
Palette: sky #6fb0ea #9cccf3 #cfe7fb; ink #15181c / #0f1218; paper #fff; red #ef4f26 / #d23a16; mute #6b7480.
Type: Archivo (display, wdth 125, 900, uppercase, tight), Inter Tight (body), JetBrains Mono (labels), Caveat (annotations).
Shapes: white cards r22 with soft blue shadow; mono kickers; stipple (dot-halftone) headline fill.
Motion: power3/expo eases, 0.5-0.9s entrances, staggered, camera pans between stations, shared-element expand into the demo footage.
