Why Liensight.

Built for the Blockchain Legal Institute's second Legal Tech Hackathon on DoraHacks. Here is the brief, and how we chose this idea.

Most builds anchored an AI's output. We wanted the chain to act on a government record.

An ACRIS filing is public, dated and checkable by anyone. That made it a better trigger than another model's opinion.

how we chose

Three ideas. Three rounds.

idea review · a contradictor critiques each round3 rounds
Round 1
A
Consent Clock

Minors' data consent that lapses with age. Critique: auto-deleting data on a missed re-consent is legally wrong.

B
Lien Tripwire

Pause a property token when a lien is recorded. Critique: "the token isn't the title".

C
Deemed-Denial Clock

Track public-records deadlines. Critique: deadlines vary and get extended; little real need.

Rounds 2 and 3
B
Lien Tripwire, revised

Reframed as SPV equity with an issuer-governed restriction. Nodes must agree on the document set, not on a summary.

✓
Verdict

Ranked first: a concrete live demo, an empty RWA track, and a clear use for CRE. Renamed Liensight.
