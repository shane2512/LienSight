reading the result

Four possible answers.

Answer 01

Restricted. Adverse.

At least one federal lien, tax lien, tax-lien sale, common-charge lien or judgment is on record. On-chain, the token would restrict transfers and cite these document IDs.

ANSWER 02
● Open

No adverse filings.

Documents exist, but none are on the adverse map. Transfers would stay open.

ANSWER 03
No documents

Nothing found.

Zero documents is more likely a wrong BBL than a spotless building. The workflow refuses to report in this case.

ANSWER 04
ACRIS error

Fail static.

If ACRIS errors or a response is cut off at 5,000 rows, the workflow writes nothing. A failed fetch can never set or clear a restriction.
