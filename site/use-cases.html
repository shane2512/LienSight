Three people who care.

A lien on the building behind a token matters to whoever issues the token, whoever holds it, and whoever checks the trades.

01 · The issuer

Runs the SPV.

Wants shares to stop changing hands while a fresh lien sits on the building, without staff checking county records every morning.
