fair questions

Ask us anything.

is-it-title.frame
01Is the token the title to the building?
No. The token is equity in a company (SPV) that owns the building. The restriction comes from the issuer's own rules, not from property law.
why-a-don.frame
02Why use a decentralized network for one public API?
So no single server decides what the chain sees. Every node reads ACRIS independently and they must agree on the exact document set. Anyone can check the cited document IDs on ACRIS.
can-it-hide.frame
03Can the issuer just hide a lien?
The issuer can clear a restriction, publicly, on-chain. It has to name the exact set of liens it reviewed. If a new lien landed in the meantime, the clear fails.
lis-pendens.frame
04Does it catch lis pendens or foreclosures?
No. Those are court and county-clerk filings, and ACRIS has no document type for them. Liensight covers federal liens, tax liens, tax-lien sales, common-charge liens and judgments.
how-fast.frame
05How fast does it notice?
The workflow runs on a schedule (every 10 minutes in our config). But ACRIS itself records filings days after the real event, so this is an early warning, not instant.
is-it-live.frame
06Is this running in production?
No. It runs on the Sepolia testnet, with reports sent by the CRE simulator through Chainlink's mock forwarder. Production needs a deployed CRE workflow and the production forwarder.
